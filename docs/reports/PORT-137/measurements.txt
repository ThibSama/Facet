PORT-137 — header geometry with the language switch, FR and EN.

Every row is measured on the served page, in the language named.

"header" is the shell row PORT-137 added a control to: it overflows
when the brand, the language switch, the collapse control and the
theme toggle no longer fit on one line. That is the measurement the
placement decision turned on, and it is "no" at every width.

"document" is the whole page scrolling sideways. It is "no" at every
width in both languages: the skills ribbons and the finale plate run
past the measure by design and are clipped, so nothing escapes.

"collision" is any pair of header controls whose boxes intersect.
